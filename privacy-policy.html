Privacy Policy, Terms & Disclaimer

At Al Manal Dental Center, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal and medical information. This document covers our privacy practices, terms under which we provide dental services, and necessary disclaimers in accordance with the laws of the United Arab Emirates.

Important: By accessing or using the services of Al Manal Dental Center, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Medical Disclaimer, and compliance statements.

1 Privacy Policy

We are fully committed to protecting your privacy by maintaining the confidentiality and security of your personal and health information.

1.1 Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal and sensitive information:

  • Personal identification such as name, age, nationality, Emirates ID, and passport.
  • Contact information including email, phone number, and mailing address.
  • Payment and billing information.
  • Dental and medical history, treatment records, X-rays, and clinical notes.
  • Any information you voluntarily provide via forms, calls, or in-person visits.

1.2 How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver safe and effective dental treatments and oral healthcare services.
  • To manage and maintain patient records and clinical data.
  • To process billing, insurance claims, and payment transactions.
  • To communicate appointment reminders, follow-ups, and treatment updates.

1.3 Data Protection & Security

  • Secure physical storage of patient files.
  • Encrypted digital systems and access controls.
  • Employee confidentiality agreements and training.
  • Compliance with UAE data protection and healthcare regulations.

1.4 Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your information. We may share data only with dental specialists involved in treatment, insurance companies for claims processing, legal or regulatory authorities when required, and with your explicit consent.

1.5 Your Rights as a Patient

  • Access and review your personal and medical records.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information.
  • Withdraw your consent for certain data uses, subject to legal obligations.
  • Request deletion of data in accordance with applicable laws.

2 Terms of Use

2.1 Scope of Services

  • We provide professional dental care, diagnostics, treatments, and consultations.
  • All treatments are provided by licensed and qualified dental professionals.
  • Services offered may vary and are customised based on individual patient needs.

2.2 Patient Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate and complete medical and personal information.
  • Arrive on time and follow pre- and post-treatment instructions.
  • Settle payment obligations promptly according to agreed terms.

2.3 Fees, Charges, and Billing

  • Treatment costs will be discussed and agreed upon before procedures.
  • Payment is due at the time of service unless otherwise arranged.
  • Insurance coverage is subject to verification, policies, and pre-approvals.

2.4 Limitation of Liability

We strive for excellent outcomes, but individual results may vary. We are not liable for complications arising from failure to follow aftercare instructions.

3 Medical Disclaimer

Information on our website, marketing materials, and consultations is for general informational purposes only and should not be considered a substitute for professional dental or medical advice.

3.1 Not a Substitute for Medical Consultation

  • Always seek the advice of a qualified healthcare provider for diagnosis and treatment.
  • Do not disregard professional medical advice based on information found on our website.
  • In case of a dental emergency, contact us immediately or visit the nearest emergency facility.

3.2 Individual Results May Vary

Testimonials, before-and-after images, and success stories shared by Al Manal Dental Center are real patient experiences. Results vary depending on individual oral health conditions, treatment plans, and other factors.

4 Compliance with UAE Regulations

Al Manal Dental Center operates in full compliance with the laws and regulations governing healthcare services in the United Arab Emirates, including UAE Federal Law, UAE Data Protection Laws, Department of Health regulations, and MOHAP directives.

5 Contact Information

If you have questions or concerns regarding this policy, please contact us.
